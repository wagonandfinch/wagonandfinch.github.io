Privacy Policy — Wagon & Finch Pin Scheduler

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Wagon & Finch Pin Scheduler is a private tool used only by Wagon & Finch to publish and manage Pins for our own Pinterest business account. It is not offered to the public or to other Pinterest users.

What data the app accesses

  • Our own Pinterest boards and Pins, so we can create and organize them.
  • Analytics for our own account and Pins, such as impressions, saves, and clicks.

The app does not access other Pinterest users' accounts, Pins, boards, or personal information.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to schedule our Pins and review how they perform. Access tokens and analytics are stored privately on systems we control. We do not sell, share, or transfer this data to anyone.

Retention and deletion

Analytics are kept only as long as they are useful for our own reporting. Revoking the app's access in Pinterest settings stops all access immediately.
